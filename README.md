# Growth Audit — Planoly

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/planoly-flow-rebuild/

Every new Planoly install gets nine posts planned before the paywall. Then “Choose your plan” shows three equal cards and never mentions them. This audit rebuilds the first launch so the paywall sells the week it just planned, in Planoly’s own design and at Planoly’s own prices.

Two views: an **Overview** with five ranked ideas and the three Meta ad promises Planoly runs today, and a **Prototype** with three phones, one per ad, that you can tap through.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Expected effect |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask for the account after the plan, not before it | CR +5–10% · ARPU +10–17% |
| 02 | Name their business in the paywall headline | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Recommend one plan and dock one button | CR +5–15% · ARPU +3–8% |
| 04 | Bring 4.7 stars and Dani K. to the decision | CR +10–15% · ARPU +2–5% |
| 05 | Make the loader read their answer back | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Planoly.

## Grounding

Screens, copy, post themes, testimonials and prices come from the Planoly iOS app as recorded on 11 November 2026 (EU storefront: Starter 19 € / 199,99 €, Growth 29 € / 299 €, Pro 59 € / 599 €, 14 days free). 4.7 from 27K ratings and “8 million creators” come from the US App Store listing. The ads come from the Meta Ad Library (page Planoly), seen 11 November 2026.
